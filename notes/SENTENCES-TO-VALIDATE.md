# Sentences to validate

**What I am asking for:** cross out what is not said, correct what is said
differently. Tick what is good.

**How they are made:** 8 seed sentences already present in your content, plus the
course's own features applied on top of them as buttons — the past, negation, the
question, "very", the change of person. **No word from outside the course.**

**The warning that matters:** I am not a Vietnamese speaker. These are candidates
built by assembly, not verified Vietnamese. A grammatical sentence nobody says is
worse than one sentence fewer — it installs itself as a reflex.

## 1 · `ăn cơm` — to eat rice

- [ ] `tôi ăn cơm` — I eat rice
- [ ] `tôi không ăn cơm` — I do not eat rice
- [ ] `tôi đã ăn cơm` — I ate rice
- [ ] `tôi ăn cơm rồi` — I have already eaten
- [ ] `tôi chưa ăn cơm` — I have not eaten yet
- [ ] `anh ăn cơm chưa?` — have you eaten? *(the everyday greeting)*
- [ ] `anh có ăn cơm không?` — do you eat rice?
- [ ] `tôi đang ăn cơm` — I am eating right now
- [ ] `tôi sẽ ăn cơm` — I will eat rice
- [ ] `tôi muốn ăn cơm` — I want to eat rice
- [ ] `tôi cũng ăn cơm` — I eat rice too
- [ ] `tôi phải ăn cơm` — I have to eat rice

## What I am not sure about at all

Set apart because they touch points where I have a real doubt:

- [ ] `anh ăn cơm chưa?` — I believe this is **the** everyday Vietnamese
      greeting, but I do not know whether it is said with `anh` or rather with
      no pronoun at all
- [ ] `cà phê ngon lắm` vs `cà phê rất ngon` — both exist, but which one should a
      beginner learn first?
- [ ] `tôi phải ăn cơm` — grammatically correct, but is it something people say?
- [ ] `tôi cũng ăn cơm` — same, it may sound artificial on its own
