These are not bad words and not bad glosses. Each is real and frequent enough
to have earned a place in a 2000-word list. None of them belongs in a
beginner's first hundred hours.

`Việt Nam` and `Tết` are deliberately NOT here — they are the course's own.

Meo already pulled one back: `ấm` (warm) was a false positive of the keyword
scan, on the substring in "warm". So were `kho` (warehouse), `cảnh báo`
(warning), `tiệc` (party), `điều tra` (to investigate) and `xuất sắc`
(excellent). All six stay in the course.
